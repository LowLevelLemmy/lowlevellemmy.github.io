Creep Catchers Privacy Policy

Lemmy LLC

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This policy applies to the paid, ad-free edition of Creep Catchers. Policies for other Lemmy LLC products are unchanged.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

Creep Catchers does not require an account, display advertisements, or offer in-app purchases. The game does not record or transmit analytics, create analytics identifiers, or send automatic crash reports. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, earned currencies and in-game subscriber count are stored on your device. The subscriber count is a game statistic, not a list of real people.

Sharing and external links

If you choose to share a gameplay screenshot and caption, the game passes them to your device’s share sheet. You select the receiving app or recipient. Social-media buttons open external services, whose own privacy policies apply. We do not receive your social-media login information.

Platform services

Your purchase of the app, and any optional platform backup or diagnostic services, are handled by the platform provider under its own policies. We do not receive your payment card information.

Changes to this policy

We will post updates to this policy on this page and update its effective date when our privacy practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact lemmy@lemmy.llc.
